# Decision: Short title

- Status: Proposed
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Owners: Names or roles
- Related issue: Link

## Claim

State the selected decision and the conditions where it applies.

## Grounds

List the implementation facts, tests, measurements, research, or accepted requirements.

## Warrant and backing

Explain why the grounds support the claim. Cite the applicable invariant, standard, or
project decision.

## Alternatives and why not

Describe the closest credible alternative. State when it would be preferable and why
the present evidence rejects it.

Include the current behavior when doing nothing is a credible alternative.

## Limits and rebuttal

State the accepted trade-off, strongest counterexample, and evidence that should reopen
the decision.

## Consequences

Describe ownership, contracts, failure behavior, recovery, operations, and maintenance.

## Validation

Name the checks or observations that can confirm the decision after implementation.
